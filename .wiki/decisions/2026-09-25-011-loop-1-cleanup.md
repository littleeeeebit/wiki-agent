# fix: loop step 1 — Organize the misalignment of plans, comments, and code, and close small deferred tasks

What. `session_state.open_steps` is determined by the first word in the status column. `완료 — 양 호스트 실측` is the completed row, `미완료 — …` is the remaining row. Write that it is a record from the public copy era at the head of `docs/verification.md` and point to the current verification (`done/wiki-agent/7-verify.md`). Remove the Korean branch of `branch_line` and the `english` argument from the "review cell" in the wiki-agent overview to loop step 4 …

Why. The focus of the next task in the later stage selects candidates from the remaining rows of SessionStart. If `완료 — …` rows are counted as remaining work, candidates come from the wrong place. The rest are things deferred from steps 6 and 7, and there was no other reason to wait. The cause of haiku answering in Korean is inside the repository. There are no user-unit `CLAUDE.md`·`language` settings, and it was Korean both times even with the hook and user settings turned off. Changing it to `--system-prompt` makes it English, but the Claude Code default prompt is lost. Adding one more line at the end resulted in English all three times in the actual combination (hook on, answer prompt + wiki focus).
